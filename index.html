HR360 Privacy Policy

For HR360, including the HR360 Go mobile application

Effective date: 27 September 2026

HR360 (“we,” “us”) is a workforce management service used by organizations and their employees. This policy explains the personal information handled through HR360 and HR360 Go, why it is used, who can access it, and how to request access or deletion. Some functions depend on the features your employer enables and the permissions you grant.

For employees: Your employer generally controls the HR and attendance records it enters or collects through HR360. HR360 provides and operates the service for that employer. Contact your employer's HR administrator about employment-record corrections or access; you may also contact HR360 using the details below.

1. Information we handle

  • Identity and employee information: name, employee ID, work contact details, employer, branch, role, job and account details, and information entered by your employer or you. Depending on subscribed modules this may include schedules, leave, tasks, payroll-related records, and documents.
  • Attendance and work activity: check-in and check-out timestamps, shift and roster details, attendance status, task activity and work-time records where enabled.
  • Location: GPS coordinates or location checks when you use location-based attendance or geofencing, and configured work-site or check-in location information.
  • Photos: check-in or check-out selfies where the employer has enabled photo verification and you use the camera feature.
  • Device and technical information: device and app identifiers, operating system, IP address, logs, error information, and security or authentication events needed to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service. NFC check-in may record the checkpoint identifier and associated attendance event where enabled.
  • Communications: information you send to support and any content you submit through enabled HR360 features.

Information may be provided by your employer, by you, or automatically when you use the service. Available fields and modules vary by employer configuration.

2. How we use information

We use this information to create and authenticate accounts; provide employee self-service and enabled HR functions; record and verify attendance, location-based check-ins, and work activity; display records to authorized employer personnel; operate payroll-related and reporting workflows where enabled; respond to support requests; secure and maintain the service; and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use employee location or selfies for advertising.

3. GPS, geofencing, and attendance

If your employer enables mobile attendance, HR360 Go may request device location when you initiate a check-in or check-out or use another location-dependent attendance action. The location can be compared with an employer-configured permitted work area, stored with the attendance event, and made available to authorized personnel at your employer. Location permission can be managed in your device settings, though denying it may prevent location-based check-in. If HR360 Go requests background location in a particular app version, that use must be separately explained in the app's permission disclosure and reflected here before release.

4. Selfie check-in and camera

If enabled by your employer, the app uses the camera to capture a selfie during attendance check-in or check-out. The photo may be linked to that attendance record so authorized employer personnel can verify the event. Camera permission can be changed in your device settings, though photo-required check-in may then be unavailable. This policy does not claim that HR360 performs facial recognition or biometric template processing; if those features are introduced, this section must be updated before use.

5. Who can access information

We make relevant records available to your employer's authorized administrators and managers according to their permissions. We may use contracted providers for hosting, storage, communications, security, and technical support, with access limited to providing those services. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users. An employer may export or otherwise use records according to its own policies and legal duties. We do not sell employee personal information.

Data may be processed in countries where HR360, your employer, or authorized service providers operate. Applicable safeguards and contract terms govern such processing.

6. Retention and security

We retain personal information while needed to provide HR360 to the employer and for applicable employment, tax, security, dispute, and legal obligations. Retention depends on the record type, the employer's instructions, and applicable law. When information is no longer required, it is deleted or de-identified under our applicable procedures; backup copies may remain temporarily until their normal expiration. We use access controls, authenticated accounts, and reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You can manage location and camera permissions through your device settings. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. For employer-controlled records, contact your employer's HR administrator; we will assist with requests as appropriate. You may also send a privacy request to HR360 using the contact method below. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

8. Request account and data deletion

To request deletion of your HR360 Go account and associated personal information, email info@tkminds.com with the subject “HR360 account deletion request.” Include the email address or employee ID associated with your account and your employer's name. Do not send passwords or identity documents in your initial email. We will verify the request and explain the next steps. You can submit this request without reinstalling the app.

Deleting an account means deleting associated data under our control, subject to records that your employer or applicable law requires us to retain, such as payroll, attendance, tax, fraud-prevention, or security records. Where a record must be kept, we will limit its use and explain the applicable retention reason in our response. Merely disabling access is not treated as account deletion. You may also ask your employer's HR administrator to initiate deletion or correct employer-managed records.

If HR360 Go allows account creation in the app, an accessible deletion request path must also be available inside the app. This webpage provides the external request path once the contact address above is replaced with an operating mailbox.
